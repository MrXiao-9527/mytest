3.2 Infrastructure and Talent Gaps

Surveyed firms report uneven digital foundations across business units. Legacy systems slow data sharing and increase integration cost.

Talent shortages in data engineering and cybersecurity remain the primary bottleneck for sustained transformation outcomes at scale.

Institutions that combine governance redesign with targeted training show stronger performance gains within 18 months.
