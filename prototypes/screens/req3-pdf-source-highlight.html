3.2 Infrastructure and Talent Gaps

Digital programs often stall when infrastructure upgrades outpace workforce readiness. Organizations may deploy platforms quickly, yet lack the specialists required to operate them securely and at scale.

Talent shortages in data engineering and cybersecurity remain the primary bottleneck for sustained transformation outcomes at scale.

Addressing this gap requires coordinated investment in training, cross-functional teams, and long-term talent pipelines aligned with campus research strengths.
