CNKI AI Personalized Prototypes

White-and-blue, international-style mockups for five campus personalization features—from identity linking and source-scoped answers to dual-column tracing, entity guidance, and AI reading.

1. Identity & Dashboard 2. Source Settings 3. Source Tracing 4. Entity Guidance 5. AI Reading
Requirement 1 · 2 screens

Campus identity & school dashboard

Auto-link on campus IP; one-tap institution link off campus; home becomes a school discovery dashboard.

  • On campus: IP detects and binds the institution
  • Off campus: link your CNKI account once
  • Dashboard shows faculty papers, new theses, and research trends
Requirement 2 · 1 screen

Source settings for scoped answers

Let users choose campus sources before asking, so generation stays within selected collections.

  • Open Source Settings under the input
  • Toggle IR, Special Collections, Department Corpus
  • Model retrieval shrinks to the selected campus scope
Requirement 3 · 2 screens

Dual-column source tracing

Grounded answer on the left; matching source cards on the right; jump to the exact PDF passage.

  • Citations with school markers in the answer
  • Hover/click a marker to highlight the source card
  • Open Source jumps to the cited paragraph
Requirement 4 · 2 screens

Entity guidance & scholar profile

Suggest people, labs, and collections as users type; open a rich scholar profile instead of a flat result list.

  • Prompt guide for authors, labs, and collections
  • Scholar profile: timeline, keywords, grants, collaboration map
Requirement 5 · 1 screen

AI reading companion

Read campus theses or special collections with a persistent assistant for summary and follow-ups.

  • Right rail: AI Summary and outline
  • Select text and Ask for context-aware follow-ups
  • Sessions save to the personal dashboard
